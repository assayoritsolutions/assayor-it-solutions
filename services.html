ASSAYOR ENGAGEMENT MODEL

Engagement Models Designed Around Your Business

Every organization operates differently. Project complexity, timelines, internal capabilities, and priorities vary. We believe the engagement model should adapt to your business—not the other way around. We recommend the best fit based on your objectives, risk profile, and investment strategy.

Fixed Price

Best suited for projects with clearly defined scope, timelines, and specific deliverables.

Ideal For:
Oracle Implementations Web Dev Mobile Apps Product Dev
Advantages:
  • Predictable project cost
  • Clearly defined deliverables
  • Structured project governance
  • Milestone-based execution
  • Lower commercial uncertainty

Time & Material (T&M)

Ideal when scope is expected to evolve. Pay only for the actual effort invested.

Best For:
Consulting Discovery Workshops Agile Delivery Advisory Services
Advantages:
  • High flexibility
  • Faster project initiation
  • Continuous prioritization
  • Better support for changing requirements

Dedicated Team

A dedicated team works exclusively as a seamless extension of your own organization.

Best For:
Digital Transformation Enterprise Programs Continuous Engineering
Advantages:
  • Consistent project knowledge
  • Long-term collaboration
  • Better productivity
  • Strong business understanding
  • Faster decision-making

Staff Augmentation

Providing experienced consultants to instantly strengthen your existing project teams.

Typical Roles:
Solution Architects Functional/Tech Consultants Project Managers Business Analysts
Advantages:
  • Rapid onboarding
  • Flexible scaling
  • Reduced recruitment effort
  • Immediate access to expertise

Managed Services

Comprehensive post-implementation support, maintenance, and continuous optimization.

Includes:
Application Support Incident Resolution Release Mgmt Performance Monitoring
Advantages:
  • Continuous system improvement
  • Guaranteed SLAs
  • Strategic technology advisory
  • Reduced internal IT burden

Our Recommendation Philosophy

One of Assayor's distinguishing characteristics is our commitment to recommending the engagement model that creates the greatest value for you. If we believe another commercial approach will better suit your objectives or reduce your investment while maintaining quality, we will recommend it—even if it means a smaller initial engagement for us. We believe long-term trust is built through honest advice, not maximizing short-term revenue.
